# Fueling plan

> Paths in this skill are relative to this skill's own folder
> (the "Base directory for this skill" shown when it loads),
> e.g. `<base>/references/coaching-principles.md`.
> Never resolve them from the plugin root. If a file is missing,
> look inside this skill's folder before continuing; do not skip it.

Read `references/coaching-principles.md`, `references/athlete-profile.md` and `references/fueling-guidelines.md` (in this skill's folder). Scope is fueling for training and racing. This command does not create daily diets or weight-loss plans, but if the athlete already has a nutrition setup (Project instructions, memory, a dietitian's plan), build the fueling plan so it fits with it.

## 1. Inputs

Use what you already have and ask once, in one message, for what is missing:

- **The ride or race:** a race from the profile, a planned session, or a route/GPX the athlete shares. From it: distance, elevation, expected duration (estimate from the athlete's recent speed on similar terrain if not given, and say it is an estimate), intensity, surface.
- **Conditions:** expected temperature. If the date is close and web search is available, check the forecast.
- **Logistics:** how many bottles and their size, where water or food is available (fountains, shops, feed zones, opening hours for long or night riding), whether it is self-supported.
- **The athlete:** what sits well and what does not, products they already use (gels, drink mix, bars, real food), body weight for per-kg figures, and whether they have trained high intakes before.

## 2. Output

Reply in the profile's language and units.

1. **Target per hour:** carbohydrate (g/h), fluid (ml/h) and sodium, with one line of reasoning (duration, intensity, heat).
2. **Plan by sections:** split the route or the duration into blocks (by hour, or by segment for routes), saying what to take in each, adjusted to terrain (eat before long climbs, not during them; fuel early, from the first hour).
3. **Bottles:** what goes in each and in what proportion (for > 60 g/h a glucose/maltodextrin:fructose mix around 2:1), where to refill.
4. **Packing list:** how many gels, bars, sandwiches, bottles, electrolyte tabs; plus spares.
5. **Before and after:** pre-ride meal and timing; snack if needed; what to take afterwards, especially if another hard day or stage follows.
6. **For multi-day and self-supported events:** resupply points and distances between them, what must be carried between them, night sections, end-of-day recovery meal and breakfast, caffeine timing that does not ruin sleep.
7. **For A races:** a gut-training progression for the long rides of the previous weeks, building up to the race intake.

Add the warnings that apply: heat, very long gaps without water, over-drinking risk in ultras, an intake the athlete has never practised.

Keep it practical enough to print and pack from.
